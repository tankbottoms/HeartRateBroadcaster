Privacy Policy

HeartRate Broadcaster collects nothing, stores nothing on a server, and has no server to store it on.

Last updated 11 August 2026.

What the app does with your data

Heart rate

Read from HealthKit on your iPhone and Apple Watch, held in memory only for as long as the app is running, and written to a Bluetooth characteristic that only devices you have paired can subscribe to. It is never written to a file, never uploaded, and is gone when the app closes.

Your settings

Broadcast name, source, cadence, and display toggles are stored in the app's own preferences on your device. They stay there. They are not part of any account, because there are no accounts.

Bluetooth

The app acts as a Bluetooth peripheral. It advertises a heart rate service and answers devices that subscribe. It does not scan for or connect to other devices, and Bluetooth gives it no identifying information about whatever subscribed.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No internet requests. Nothing is sent off your own network.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

The virtual shifter

The one part of the app that touches a network is the virtual shifter, and it is off until you switch it on. Switched on, the app listens on your own Wi-Fi network and announces itself over Bonjour so a cycling app on the same network can connect to it and receive shift commands. The connection is between your two devices only. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, no account is involved, and switching the shifter off closes the listener.

Health data

Heart rate read through HealthKit is used solely to display it and to broadcast it to devices you pair. It is not used for advertising or marketing, is not shared with third parties, and is not disclosed to data brokers. You can revoke the app's HealthKit access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health, or in the Health app under Sharing › Apps. Revoking it stops the readings; nothing is retained.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.
